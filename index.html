The E-mployee Operating Model plugin turns the leadership instruments of Dr. Jonah Tebaa’s book into guided workflows inside ChatGPT. It helps CEOs and executives decide which AI work deserves a defined role, then design how that work is directed, governed, measured and owned. Each workflow ends in a table, a verdict and a list of gaps a leadership team can take into a decision meeting.

Open the plugin in ChatGPT

The fifteen workflows

  1. Get started: routes you to the right workflow
  2. AI access audit (chapters 1–2)
  3. E-mployee inclusion test (chapter 3)
  4. E-mployer capability audit (chapter 4)
  5. Outcome-first design (chapter 5)
  6. E-mployee Role Charter, 13 fields (chapter 6)
  7. Value Ledger (chapter 7)
  8. First-role selection with the Opportunity Matrix (chapter 8)
  9. Autonomy review on the Autonomy Ladder (chapter 9)
  10. Authority Matrix (chapter 10)
  11. Incident accountability (chapter 11)
  12. Risk classification (chapter 12)
  13. Human + AI org chart (chapter 13)
  14. Portfolio review (chapter 14)
  15. Gated 90-day roadmap (chapter 15)
